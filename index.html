How to play

  1. The crib. You are dealt six cards. Keep four and send two to the crib, an extra hand that belongs to the dealer.
  2. The starter. One card is turned up and counts as part of every hand. If it is a Jack, the dealer scores 2.
  3. The play. Take turns laying cards and adding to a running count that may not pass 31. Score 2 for making 15 or 31, 2, 6 or 12 for a pair, three or four of a kind, and 1 per card for a run of three or more. If you cannot play, your opponent carries on, and the last card before the count resets scores 1.
  4. The show. Each hand is counted with the starter, the non-dealer first, then the dealer, then the crib. Every combination adding to 15 scores 2, each pair 2, each run 1 per card, a flush 4 or 5, and a Jack matching the starter's suit 1.
  5. Winning. The first peg to reach the last hole wins at once, even in the middle of a hand.

Tap a card to choose it. During the play, tap it again or press Play. On a keyboard: 1–6 choose a card, Enter confirms, H asks for a hint.

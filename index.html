How to play

  1. The crib. You are dealt six cards. Keep four and send two to the crib, an extra hand that belongs to the dealer.
  2. The starter. One card is turned up and counts as part of every hand. If it is a Jack, the dealer scores 2.
  3. The play. Take turns laying cards and adding to a running count that may not pass 31. Score 2 for making 15 or 31, 2, 6 or 12 for a pair, three or four of a kind, and 1 per card for a run of three or more. If none of your cards fits, press Go: the others carry on, and the last card before the count resets scores 1.
  4. The show. Each hand is counted with the starter, from the dealer's left round to the dealer, then the crib. Every combination adding to 15 scores 2, each pair 2, each run 1 per card, a flush 4 or 5, and a Jack matching the starter's suit 1.
  5. Three or four players. Each is dealt five cards and sends one to the crib; with three, one more card goes to the crib from the deck. Three play each for themselves, on a board with three lanes. Four play as two partnerships, players 1 and 3 against 2 and 4, each pair sharing a score.
  6. Winning. The first peg to reach the last hole wins at once, even in the middle of a hand.

Tap a card to choose it. During the play, tap it again or press Play. Tap anywhere to skip the deal, a count or a celebration. On a keyboard: 1–6 choose a card, Enter confirms, H asks for a hint and M mutes the sound.
